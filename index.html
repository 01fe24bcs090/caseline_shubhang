Capabilities

Built for the
toughest investigations.

Every feature is designed to preserve evidence integrity while accelerating analysis. No black boxes — every AI decision is auditable.

🎯

Person Detection & Tracking

YOLO11 + ByteTrack assigns persistent IDs to every individual across frames with blue bounding box overlays.

🔗

Cross-Camera Re-ID

MobileNetV3 appearance embeddings + cosine similarity match the same person across different camera views automatically.

✂️

Smart Video Trimming

Output videos start the moment a person enters the frame and end when they leave — zero wasted footage.

👁️

Analysis Pipeline

From raw CCTV
to courtroom-ready evidence.

A transparent, five-step pipeline where AI handles the heavy lifting and humans make every critical decision.

01

Ingest & Hash

Video feeds are ingested and SHA-256 hashed for tamper-proof chain of custody. Every frame is accounted for.

Automated
02

Detect & Track

YOLO11 detects persons per frame. ByteTrack maintains persistent IDs through occlusions and re-entries.

AI-Powered
03

Extract Appearance

MobileNetV3 encodes each subject into a 576-dim feature vector. Sampled crops are saved as visual evidence.

AI-Powered
04

Cross-Camera Matching

Candidate identity links are proposed by cosine similarity and temporal analysis — then surfaced for human review.

Human Review
05

Report & Export

Approved links generate an incident timeline, path graph, and a downloadable PDF evidence report.
